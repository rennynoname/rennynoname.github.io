I am a philosopher with an MA from Central European University in Vienna. My work sits at the intersection of philosophy of mind, metaphysics, and philosophy of physics: I am interested in the self and the subjectivity of experience, and in what the first-person point of view means for our attempts to give an objective account of reality, including questions about time and self-locating belief in cosmology.

Before returning to graduate study, I spent several years working directly with people rather than only writing about them: as a wilderness therapy field guide, a conservation corps crew leader, and a child welfare caseworker. I write about both sides of that work, academic and otherwise, and you can find more on the About and Research pages, in my CV, and in a longer-running journal of essays on travel and everyday life.
