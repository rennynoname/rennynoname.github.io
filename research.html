Current Thesis

Understanding the First- and Third-Person Viewpoint MA Thesis · Central European University, Vienna · supervised by Prof. Simon Rippon · expected defense, September 2026

An inquiry into the relationship between subjective, first-person experience and the objective, third-person descriptions we use to talk about minds, and what each can and cannot capture about the other.

Research Interests

My interests sit at the intersection of philosophy of mind, epistemology, and the philosophy of science. I am particularly drawn to an interdisciplinary approach that treats findings from the natural sciences, astronomy especially, as a resource for philosophical theorizing about knowledge, subjectivity, and our place in the universe, rather than treating the two as separate enterprises.
